Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This privacy policy describes how ThreadsPageManager ("we", "our", or "us") collects and uses information through our Facebook application.

The App integrates with Meta's Threads platform to allow authorized users to connect their Threads account and publish content through the Threads API.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the App to connect to your Threads account, the App may access information made available through the permissions you grant to it, including:

  • Your Threads account identifier.
  • Your Threads username and basic profile information.
  • Content and information necessary to publish posts through the Threads API.
  • Access tokens provided by Meta to authorize API requests.

We only request and use information that is necessary to provide the functionality of the App.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the Threads API is used only to operate and provide the App's functionality, including:

  • Connecting your Threads account to the App.
  • Authenticating requests to the Threads API.
  • Creating and publishing Threads posts at your request.
  • Maintaining the functionality and security of the App.

We do not use Threads account information for advertising, profiling, selling personal information, or any purpose unrelated to the functionality described above.

3. Data Storage

We do not intentionally store your Threads posts or Threads profile information longer than necessary to provide the App's functionality.

If access tokens or other information required for API authentication are stored, they are stored only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and are protected using reasonable security measures.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

The App communicates with Meta's Threads API in order to provide its functionality. Information transmitted to Meta is handled according to Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Meta Platforms

The App uses Meta's Threads API. Your use of Threads remains subject to Meta's applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

You can review Meta's privacy policy at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policies/ .

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the App against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Revoking Access

You may stop the App from accessing your Threads account by revoking the App's authorization through the relevant Meta/Threads account settings.

If you revoke authorization, the App will no longer be able to make authorized requests to the Threads API on your behalf.

8. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the App by contacting us at the email address below.

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will delete applicable personal information that we control, except where we are required to retain information for legal, security, or legitimate business purposes.

Revoking the App's access to your Threads account does not necessarily delete information that has already been stored independently of the Threads API. To request deletion of such information, please contact us.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of your information, please contact: hi@nnnicolae.com
